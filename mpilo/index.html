01

Executive Proposition

Mpilo’s guest experience depends on more than comfortable rooms and welcoming service. For a boutique hotel in central Maseru with 26 rooms and two suites, the Sky Restaurant, a wine bar and three conference venues, reliable connectivity, working front-desk devices, dependable booking and payment processes, secure information and well-run conference technology all help staff deliver the experience guests expect.

iNSIGHTS proposes a practical hybrid IT support partnership combining remote assistance with planned and needs-based onsite support.

Core Objective: The goal is to reduce avoidable disruption, resolve everyday technology issues faster, improve visibility of recurring problems, and provide a clear support route for the whole property. Final scope and pricing should be confirmed after an assessment of sites, users, devices, systems, connectivity and current supplier arrangements.

Proposed Areas of Support

Click on any operational area below to examine the specific support coverage included in our partnership framework.

Front Desk & Office Tech

Troubleshoot supported computers, laptops, printers, scanners, email, user access and common software issues.

Wi-Fi, Network & CCTV Links

Complimentary guest Wi-Fi in the rooms and suites, Sky Restaurant, wine bar and conference venues; in-room Smart TV connectivity, router/access-point issues and ISP escalations. Connectivity checks for CCTV or alarm equipment where installed.

Reservations & Booking

Help with account access and the booking flow on your website and online booking engines; coordinate with the booking-platform vendors for platform faults, subject to permissions.

Payments & POS Equipment

Support card machines and POS devices at reception, the Sky Restaurant and wine bar; coordinate with payment providers. Financial transactions remain under Mpilo/provider control.

Conference & AV Tech

Pre-event checks of audio-visual equipment, screens, projectors, sound and Wi-Fi in the three conference venues, according to an agreed checklist.

Cybersecurity & Accounts

Support MFA where available, account hygiene, patching coordination, endpoint-protection checks and staff awareness.

Backups & Continuity

Review backup reports where accessible and document recovery contacts and escalation steps. Backup tools/storage separately scoped.

Website & Digital Presence

Website updates, upgrades and content changes are included, along with keeping contact details and booking pages accurate. We coordinate with your website platform provider and management team where needed; hosting and domain costs excluded unless quoted.

03

Hybrid Support: Remote + Onsite

We combine rapid remote resolution with planned physical interventions for optimal efficiency.

Channel Best Suited For How It Works
Remote User access, email/software issues, configuration guidance, vendor follow-up and issues diagnosable online. Phone, email, video call or secure remote access with prior authorisation.
Onsite Physical device faults, Wi-Fi/network checks, printer setup, conference-room tests and site assessments. Planned or priority-based visits, subject to package allowance, location and technician availability.
Events & peaks Conferences, workshops, functions and busy weekends, when downtime costs the most. Pre-event checks and agreed standby contacts, plus a shared issue log and a monthly summary of trends and recommendations.

* Remote support will be the first response where practical; onsite support will be used when hands-on intervention is needed. As Mpilo is in central Maseru, onsite visits follow the package allowance; travel beyond Maseru must be scheduled and priced transparently in the final agreement.

Support Workflow

  1. Log: Site, affected service/device, business impact, contact and time reported.
  2. Triage: Separate critical disruption from standard requests and low-impact issues.
  3. Resolve Remotely: Apply fixes with approval before any remote access.
  4. Escalate: Coordinate with supplier or arrange onsite attendance if necessary.
  5. Confirm & Report: Verify recovery, record outcome, and provide monthly summaries.
