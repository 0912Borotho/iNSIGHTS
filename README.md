## 💼 Overview

**iNSIGHTS BI & Analytics (Pty) Ltd** is a Lesotho-based technology consultancy specializing in end-to-end data reporting, custom analytics dashboards, and digital maturity strategies. Founded in 2025, we enable businesses, public enterprises, and growing startups to shift from fragmented operational data to evidence-based decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Core Competencies

### 📊 Business Intelligence & Analytics
- **Power BI Development:** Custom interactive dashboards, financial models, and operational KPI tracking.
- **Data Storytelling:** Translating multi-source raw datasets into visual narratives for executive leadership.
- **Embedded Analytics:** Integrating live reports directly into client portals, web apps, and internal systems.
- **Data Pipeline Automation:** ETL processes to consolidate disparate spreadsheets and databases.

### 🚀 Digital Transformation & Advisory
- **Maturity Reviews & Strategy:** Mapping digital transformation roadmaps and technology investments.
- **Process Digitalization:** Identifying manual bottlenecks and automating core business workflows.
- **Change Enablement:** Training, capacity building, and organizational adoption.

## 🤝 Collaboration & Partnership Opportunities

We actively seek strategic alliances and collaborative engagements across industry, government, and the broader tech ecosystem:

- **White-Label BI & Analytics:** Partnering with software agencies, web development studios, and IT firms to deliver custom dashboards and embedded analytics for their clients.
- **Technical Subcontracting & Co-Delivery:** Joining forces with enterprise consultancies on large-scale data engineering, Power Platform, or digital transformation projects.
- **Public Sector & Institutional Programs:** Collaborating with government bodies, NGOs, and international development agencies on data-driven policy initiatives, capacity building, and digital maturity assessments.
- **Vendor & Technology Ecosystem Alliances:** Working alongside software vendors and cloud providers (Microsoft, AWS, Azure ecosystem) to implement modern data solutions.
- **Data Integration & API Partners:** Partnering with SaaS vendors to build out automated data connectors and reporting integrations for end-users.
